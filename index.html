St. Veronica’s Garments Ordering Policies

1. Price Structure

The price for your order is based on the cost of wholesale items, plus any additional materials or services required.

Wholesale Price: The base cost of the garment.

Supplies: This includes all materials such as ink, film paper, and other production-related items.

2. Production Turnaround

Production time is based on the size of the order:

50 items or less: 10 days

51-100 items: 2 weeks

101+ items: 3 weeks

200+ items: 1 month in advance

These timelines are estimates and may vary depending on the complexity of the order.

3. Payment Terms

Payment Method: Payment is CASH or Zelle.

Deposit Requirement: A 50% deposit is required upon signing the invoice to begin processing your order.

Order Confirmation: Your order is not confirmed until the invoice is signed and the deposit is paid.

Deposits are non-refundable.

4. Final Delivery and Pickup

Once your order is completed, you will be notified to arrange pickup. The balance of your invoice is due upon pickup in CASH or through Zelle.

5. Disclaimer

By signing the invoice and submitting your deposit, you agree to these Terms and Conditions, which govern all aspects of your order, including pricing, payment, and production timelines.

St. Veronica’s Garments reserves the right to amend these policies at any time, with changes taking effect immediately upon publication.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before signing the invoice. We are committed to providing high-quality garments and excellent service.
